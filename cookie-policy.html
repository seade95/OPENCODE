Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They help websites remember your preferences, authenticate your session, and understand how you use the site.

2. Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: Required for the Platform to function. They enable user authentication, session management, and security features. No consent required.

  • eduverse_session — maintains your login session
  • XSRF-TOKEN — prevents cross-site request forgery

Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences (theme, language).

  • eduverse_theme — stores dark/light mode preference
  • cookie_consent — records your cookie preference

Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how the Platform is used. Only placed with your consent.

  • We use privacy respecting, anonymized analytics that do not track individuals across sites

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use payment processors (e.g., Paystack) that set their own cookies. We do not control third-party cookies. Please review their respective policies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. You may block all cookies, but essential cookies are required for the Platform to function. To withdraw consent, clear your cookies and reload the site to see the consent banner again.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.

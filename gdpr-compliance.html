GDPR Compliance

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Data Processing Agreement

EduVerse acts as a data processor for school data and a data controller for user account data. We process personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679.

Your Rights

  • Right to Access — Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you. Email privacy@eduverse.app with subject "Data Access Request". We will respond within 30 days.
  • Right to Rectification — Correct inaccurate or incomplete data via your account settings or by contacting us.
  • Right to Erasure — Request deletion of your account and associated data. We will delete within 30 days unless retention is required by law.
  • Right to Restrict Processing — Limit how we use your data while a dispute is being resolved.
  • Right to Data Portability — Export your data in JSON format. Use the export feature in your account dashboard or contact us.
  • Right to Object — Object to processing for direct marketing or legitimate interests.

Data We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone, school affiliation
  • Student data: name, class, academic records, attendance (uploaded by schools)
  • Usage data: page views, feature usage, session duration (anonymized where possible)
  • Cookies: essential session cookies, optional analytics cookies with consent

Data Retention

Account data is retained until account deletion. School-provided data is retained for the duration of the service agreement plus 90 days. Anonymized analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

Data Transfers

Data may be stored on servers in the EU, US, or Africa depending on your region. We ensure adequate safeguards via Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

Third-Party Processors

  • Netlify — hosting and CDN (US/EU servers)
  • Paystack — payment processing (Nigeria)
  • Stripe — payment processing (US)

Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify affected users within 72 hours via email and publish a notice on our website.

Data Protection Officer

Contact our DPO for all GDPR-related inquiries:
Email: dpo@eduverse.app
Response time: within 48 hours

Supervisory Authority

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe we have not processed your data in compliance with GDPR.

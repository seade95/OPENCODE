Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrade anytime for immediate access to new features. Downgrade at next billing cycle.
Is there a free trial for paid plans?
The Professional plan includes a 14 day free trial. No credit card required. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept card payments via Paystack (NGN) and Stripe (USD). Bank transfers available for annual plans.
Can I export my data if I leave?
Absolutely. Export all your data (CSV/PDF) at any time. Your data belongs to you.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits?
Yes. We offer a 30% discount for registered non-profit educational institutions. Contact us for details.
How does the annual billing work?
Annual billing gives you a 20% discount compared to monthly. You pay once for the full year at the reduced rate.

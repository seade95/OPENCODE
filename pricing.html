Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect immediately on upgrade and at the next billing cycle on downgrade.
Is there a free trial for paid plans?
The Pro plan includes a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept card payments via Paystack (NGN) and Stripe (USD). Bank transfers and mobile money are available for annual plans.
Can I export my data if I leave?
Absolutely. You can export all your data (CSV/PDF) at any time. We believe in data portability — your data belongs to you.

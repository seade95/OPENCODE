Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Introduction

EduVerse ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of school administrators, teachers, students, and parents who use our school management and learning platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

Account & School Information: School name, address, administrator contact details, staff profiles, and billing information.

Student & Academic Records: Names, grade levels, attendance logs, examination scores, assessment feedback, and guardian contact details provided by participating institutions.

Usage Data: Device browser type, IP addresses, access timestamps, and telemetry logs used exclusively to ensure system performance, security, and offline data sync reliability.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and optimize all-in-one school management services.
  • To process fee transactions securely via integrated payment partners (Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe).
  • To facilitate real-time communication between teachers, students, and parents.
  • To generate automated report cards, predictive academic analytics, and attendance alerts.

4. Data Protection & Security

We enforce end-to-end encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and AES-256 encryption at rest. Multi-tenant database isolation ensures strict data partitioning between institution accounts with granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

5. Third-Party Services

EduVerse does not sell student or institutional data to third parties. Data is shared strictly with essential service infrastructure providers (hosting, payment gateways) under stringent confidentiality agreements.

6. Your Rights

School administrators and users have the right to request data access, correction, export (in CSV/JSON formats), or full account deletion. Contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@eduverse.app.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email privacy@eduverse.app or contact@eduverse.app.

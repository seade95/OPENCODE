Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when registering a school or using the platform: school name, admin contact details (name, email, phone), student and teacher records uploaded by school administrators, and payment transaction data.

We also automatically collect technical data such as browser type, device information, IP address, and usage patterns to improve the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the EduVerse platform
  • To process subscription payments and send invoices
  • To communicate platform updates, security notices, and support messages
  • To aggregate anonymized usage statistics
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage & Retention

School data is stored securely and retained for as long as the account is active. Upon account termination, data is deleted within 30 days unless retention is required by law. Backups are retained for up to 90 days.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share data with trusted third-party service providers (cloud hosting, payment processors) who are contractually bound to protect it. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data. Contact us at privacy@eduverse.app to exercise these rights.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. Analytics cookies are used only with your consent. See our Cookie Policy for details.

7. Security

We implement industry standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS), secure password hashing, and regular security audits. However, no system is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified via email or platform notice. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

For privacy inquiries: privacy@eduverse.app
